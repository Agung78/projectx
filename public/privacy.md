# Privacy Policy & Data Confidentiality

**Last Updated: October 2026**

NewCo treats client confidentiality, source code security, and architecture information as core operational responsibilities.

## Core Privacy Principles

1. **Information Collected:** Details submitted through our intake form are used exclusively to assess partnership fit. We never sell, rent, or trade contact information.
2. **Architecture Confidentiality:** System diagrams, credential mappings, and infrastructure configurations shared with NewCo are treated as strictly confidential under mutual NDA.
3. **AI Governance:** Client source code, documents, and data are never used to train or fine-tune public or third-party AI models without explicit written permission.
4. **Data Retention:** Non-contracted inquiries are deleted within 12 months. Engagement materials are returned or securely purged following handover completion.

## Legal Disclaimer
This policy is published for transparency and informational purposes only and does not constitute formal legal counsel.
