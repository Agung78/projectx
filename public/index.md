# Kyron Engineering — Engineering Independence

> Systems built properly, documented fully, and owned by the business.

Kyron Engineering partners with your team to design, build, and operate core technology systems. We do not operate as an isolated agency; our engineers join your sprints, establish shared standards, and transfer complete operational ownership before engagement conclusion.

## Core Commitments

1. **Keep it simple** — No extra complexity until there is a verified business reason.
2. **Operations included** — Monitoring, backups, cloud cost governance, and recovery plans are standard.
3. **Clear communication** — Plain-language technical updates with no jargon.
4. **Proven technology** — Standard, well-supported open-source and cloud infrastructure.
5. **Security from day one** — Access controls, encryption, and regular vulnerability screening.
